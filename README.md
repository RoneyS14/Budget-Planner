# Budget Planner

A monthly budget planner for tracking money in GBP (£), built from a personal budgeting spreadsheet.

**Live app:** https://roneys14.github.io/Budget-Planner/

## What it does

- Plan and track each month's budget across categories: Income, Fixed Bills, Variable Bills, Allowances, Savings, Investments, Subscriptions, Direct Debits and O/S Payments.
- Add your own sub-categories under each one (for example ISA, LISA and Cash under Savings), with a £ amount for each.
- Savings and Investments show a running **Total to date**, which adds up each month's allocation.
- Subscriptions record frequency (monthly or yearly), renewal date and cost. Direct Debits record an expiry date.
- A summary shows income, planned spending and what is left to assign, plus a pie chart splitting your money into Bills, Allowances, Savings, Investments and O/S Payments.
- Switch between months, copy the previous month's figures forward, or clear a month.

## Privacy

Everything is saved in your own browser. Nothing is sent to a server, so your figures stay private to you.

## Using it

Open the live link, or download `index.html` and open it in any browser.
